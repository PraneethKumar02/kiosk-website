TMA Trolley for LCA AF MK2

SBU

The TMA Trolley is designed to carry weapons and ammunition for the LCA-Tejas Aircraft.
It can transport six types of bombs, including various weights of Griffin LGB, GP, PF, and HSLD bombs.
The trolley features a leaf spring suspension system for all four wheels.
It includes a telescopic, A-frame tow bar with an arrangement to prevent braking during reverse towing.
The trolley is built for easy handling and efficient transportation of armaments.

Technical Specifications

Specification Parameter
Load Carrying Capacity Maximum 3 Tons
Towing Speed Maximum 25 km/hr
Front Axle Steerable
Braking System Hydraulic Service Brake for all wheels and Manual Parking Brake for Rear Wheel
Tow Bar A frame construction and telescopic type with arrangement to prevent braking during reverse towing
