ISO Grid Panels LVM3

SBU Aerospace

Iso grid panels are used in aerospace and space structures like panels and bulkheads.
They provide strength, rigidity, and low weight as the outer shell.
Panels are machined from metal plates with triangular pockets for stiffening ribs.
CNC rolling machines shape the panels, with isotropic-like properties.
Required facilities include high-speed profilers, CNC rollers, and skilled rolling.
