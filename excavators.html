BE1800E Electric Hydraulic Excavator

SBU Mining

  • Electric-drive twin 300 kW induction motors power the BE1800E for heavy mining duty.
  • Designed for approx. 170 ton operating mass in backhoe configuration (~175 ton in loading-shovel mode)
  • Bucket capacity ranges from ~10.5 m³ to 12.0 m³ (backhoe) and ~7.5 m³ to 12.0 m³ (loading shovel).
  • Indigenous electric hydraulic excavator aimed at mining use, yielding lower operating cost and emission-free operation for underground/large scale mining..
