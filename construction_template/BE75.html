Excavator BE75

  • Kirloskar Oil Engines Ltd. : 4R 1040 NA & 4R 1040 TC BSIII - Diesel, 4 stroke, Water Cooled, Direct Injection with natural aspirated & turbo charged.
  • Power Shift Transmission for smooth power shifting and make instant speed and directions changes by a single lever.
  • Flywheel Power (FHP): Natural Aspirated Engine: 41.9 kW (57 hp) @2000 rpm, Turbo charged Engine: 53.0 kW (71 hp) @ 2200 rpm, Bucket Capacity: 0.3 m², Operating Mass: 7500 Kg
  • Work equipment controls are simple and light touch due to the hydraulically assisted control system.
  • Hydraulic system with axial piston pump assures smooth compound work equipment movement and travel and Rugged closed center load sensing hydraulic system.
  • Naturally Aspirated Diesel engine delivers 57 FHP @2000 rpm, Turbo charged Diesel Engine delivered 71 HP (Gross) @2200 rpm. A direct injection system for reduced fuel consumption assures high operational economy.
  • Specially designed cabin provides plenty of work space and excellent visibility. Remote location of hydraulic tank away from the cabin prevents heating up of the cabin enhancing operator comfort and productivity.
  • Dual Speed hydrostatic drive with counter balance valve and automatic parking brake ensures smooth and safe operation. The lower speed can be used during excavator operation and higher speed during long distance travel for better mobility.
