Abhinandan HNX50

SBU Aerospace

Abhinandan HNX50 is a runway-free RPAS developed by BEML and VTOL Aviation India Pvt Ltd.
The UAV is a 50 kg class, fixed-wing aircraft with vertical take-off and landing capability.
It operates in both low and high-altitude regions for ISR (Intelligence, Surveillance, and Reconnaissance) applications.
Key applications include intelligence, reconnaissance, and surveillance.
Target customers include the Indian Army, Navy, BSF, Coast Guard, and CRPF.

Technical Specifications

Specification Parameter
Weight Class 50 kg
Engine Electric VTOL and IC Engine for cruise
Cruise Speed 24 - 35 m/s
