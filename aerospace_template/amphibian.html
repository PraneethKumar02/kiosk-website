Long Reach Excavator

  • The Amphibian Excavator is a compact, lightweight, and versatile machine designed for operation in marshy, swampy and waterlogged terrains where conventional excavators cannot operate. Its amphibious undercarriage allows seamless mobility over soft ground, shallow water, and wetlands, ensuring stability and safety during operations.
