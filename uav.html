BEML Tactical UAV

SBU Aerospace

BEML has ventured into the UAV sector for defense and civil applications.
The company has partnered with IIT Kanpur for joint UAV development.
It serves applications like intelligence, reconnaissance, surveillance, border patrolling, and disaster management.
Target customers include the Indian Army, Navy, BSF, Coast Guard, and CRPF.
