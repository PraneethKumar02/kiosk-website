CMV - Catenary Maintenance Vehicle

  • Meant for periodic inspection, patrolling and maintenance of overhead equipment on electrified broad/standard gauge rail routes of Metro's.
  • Used to attend sites of breakdown of overhead equipment.
  • This CMV is suitable to use in under ground.
  • Capable of erecting and restoration of damaged small lengths of catenary and contact wires.
