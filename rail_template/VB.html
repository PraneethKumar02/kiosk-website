Vande Bharat

  • The train consists of 16 coaches which includes 11 AC three-tier, four AC two-tier, and one AC first-class coach, offering a total of 823 berths.
  • The trainsets also feature jerk-free semi-permanent couplers and anti-climbers, along with a crash-worthy car-body design that is in line with EN standards.
  • The trainset is capable of running at 160 kmph with advanced safety features, including crash-worthy design elements.
  • The sleeper version is equipped with world-class amenities such as integrated reading lights with USB charging, public announcement and visual information systems, modular pantries, and special berths and toilets for differently-abled passengers.
  • The first AC coach will also offer showers with hot water, enhancing passenger comfort on long journeys.
  • The coach is provided with CCTV surveillance for the safety and security of passengers.
  • The train has automatic doors, cushioned berths, End wall fire barrier door and onboard Wi Fi.
  • Touch-Free Bio-Vacuum Toilets: These facilities ensure hygiene and convenience.
  • Passengers with restricted mobility (PRM) will be provided special accommodation and accessible toilets in the driving coaches on both ends of the train.
  • For passengers, there is also an emergency talk-balk unit connecting them to the loco pilots in case of emergencies.
  • The train also includes the "Kavach" anti-collision system and anti-climbing technology, which prevent coaches from piling on top of each other during accidents.
  • The new trains are also fire and safety-compliant, conforming to EN-45545 HL3 fire safety standards.
