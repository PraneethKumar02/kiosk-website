8 Wheeler OHE CAR

  • Meant for periodic inspection, patrolling and maintenance of overhead equipment on electrified broad gauge rail routes.
  • Used to attend sites of breakdown of overhead equipment.
  • Drivers Cab at both ends
  • Capable of erecting and restoration of damaged small lengths of catenary and contact wires.
  • Staff cabins
  • Kitchenette
  • Lifting and swiveling Platform(Hydraulic)
  • Observation Dome
  • Roof mounted pantograph for checking OHE parameters
  • Intercom facility between driver cab and lifting platform
