MECHANICAL MUNITIONS LAYER-SELF PROPELLED HMV 8x8

  • High Mobility Vehicle HMV 8X8 for Mechanical Munitions layer-Self Propelled (MML-SP) to lay/bury Bar mines in soil at various depths.
