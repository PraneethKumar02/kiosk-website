Universal Bomb Pallete

The Universal Bomb Pallet is designed for transit and storage of various bomb types, made from aluminum alloy and coated with PU paint.
Features wear-resistant rubber padding, shock-absorbing rubber abutments, and sift straps for securing stores.
Includes provisions for forklift handling and hoisting by an overhead crane.
Offers safe handling, easy road movement, and is rust-free with long life and serviceability.
Reusable after calibration, easy to load onto trucks, and designed for maintenance-free usage.

Technical Specifications

Specification Parameter
Load Carrying Capacity Minimum 1500 kg
Proof Load Minimum 2250 kg
Structural Material Aluminum Alloy AL 6061 - T6 (Rust-Resistant)
Dimensions 1500 mm x 1200 mm x 1550 mm
Weight of Pallet Maximum 110 kg
