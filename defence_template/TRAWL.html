TRAWL T-72

  • The TRAWL is a Mine breaching equipment attached to the front side of host vehicle (T72/T90 tank).
  • TRAWL is a combination of TRAWL Roller and Track Width Mine Plough (TWMP) used either together or independently depending on the requirement.
  • Additionally two nos. of Electro Magnetic Device (EMD) are fitted at the front to detonate magnetically influenced mines.
  • Roller system consists of set of three heavy rollers on either side of the host venicie fitted at vehicle front at a distance of 3.5m which exerts adequate pressure on buried mines to detonate them much ahead of tank to avoid damage to the tank and crew.
  • The roller system is designed to withstand four explosions. The TWMP is fitted at front of host vehicle's track on either side.
  • The TWMP is operated by hydraulic actuators and required hydraulic supply is tapped from the tank.
