HMV 6x6

  • BEML High mobility vehicle HMV 6x6 is used for General Service role in Indian Army. This is an all terrain vehicle intended for transport of personnel. The vehicle is also used for towing trailers weighing up to 65t on public roads and up to l6t on cross country roads. Planetary wheel hub reductions, independent wheel suspension with swinging semi-axles, axles sprung by Leaf spring type on rear axles, torsion bars and telescopic shock absorbers on front axle. The vehicle can be customised for different applications.
