HMV 8x8

SBU High Mobility Vehicles

  • BEML High mobility vehicle HMV 8x8 is an all-terrain tactical, Heavy-duty vehicle designed for rough terrain, difficult climatic and environment conditions, which can be used for mounting superstructures such as RADAR/Missile systems, Bridge laying system, etc., for Indian Army. 8x8 drive all differentials lockable, Planetary wheel hub reductions, Independent wheel suspension with swinging semi-axles and axles sprung by Leaf spring type on both front and rear axles and telescopic shock absorbers on front axle. The equipment is provided with a winch for self- recovery and recovery of other vehicles. The vehicle can be customized for different applications.
