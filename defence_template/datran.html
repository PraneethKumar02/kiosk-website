1500HP Engine for Main Battle Tanks

  • The BEML 1500HP engine, branded as the DATRAN 1500, is a significant milestone in India's defense self-reliance efforts.
  • Developed in collaboration with DRDO's CVRDE and international partner Ricardo, the engine boasts 90% indigenous content and is designed to power future Main Battle Tanks (MBTs) like the Arjun Mk-2.
  • Following the tests, the engine will be integrated into an Arjun MBT prototype for mobility trials, with full-scale production anticipated by late 2027.
